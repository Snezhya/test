HU TAO

Hu Tao serves as the 77th Director of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor in Liyue, carrying a balance of playfulness and depth that few can match. Despite her connection to the world of spirits and funerals, she embraces life with unmatched energy and optimism. Her cheerful personality often hides a sharp mind and a deep understanding of life and death—concepts she treats with both respect and humor. Beyond her mischievous charm, Hu Tao is a powerful Pyro user capable of converting her own life force into devastating attacks. She carries not only strength, but also wisdom, compassion, and a unique perspective on the cycle of existence, making her one of Liyue’s most intriguing figures.
